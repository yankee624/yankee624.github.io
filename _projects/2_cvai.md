## Design Guidelines for Continuous Vision AI (CVAI)

A comprehensive study on identifying and addressing pain points in Continuous Vision AI systems through systematic design guidelines and user studies.

### Research Contributions

- **Taxonomy Development**: Developed a comprehensive taxonomy of pain points across the entire CVAI pipeline, covering sensing, AI processing, and interaction components
- **Design Guidelines**: Derived evidence-based design guidelines for building effective CVAI applications
- **Participatory Design**: Organized design workshops to gather insights from domain experts and end users

### Implementation

Built two functional Android prototypes to validate the design guidelines:

1. **Text Recognizer**: Real-time text detection and recognition application
2. **Smart Yoga Teacher**: Intelligent posture correction system using computer vision

### Evaluation

- **Expert Review**: Conducted thorough evaluation with domain experts in AI and mobile systems
- **User Study**: Performed user studies to validate the effectiveness and usability of the design guidelines

### Impact

The research provides practical guidance for researchers and practitioners developing continuous vision AI applications, addressing common challenges in sensing, processing, and user interaction.
